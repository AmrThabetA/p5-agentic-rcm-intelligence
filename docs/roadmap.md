# Roadmap

## Gen1 — Governed agent foundation

**Status: Implemented**

The first generation establishes the governed agent loop, six typed tools, read-only semantic queries, deterministic compliance checks, recoverability ranking, role boundaries, chart-spec validation, and fail-closed numeric grounding.

## Gen2 — Configuration-driven portability

**Status: Implemented**

The second generation supports governed onboarding of configured extracts through validation, artifact and truth generation, dataset/configuration stamping, dry-run evaluation, acceptance gates, and rollback.

This is configuration-driven portability, not autonomous interpretation of an unknown schema. Adaptive table-role, grain, field-semantic, and relationship inference remains future work.

## Pre-production product baseline

**Status: Designed/not implemented**

The intended baseline adds the application/API layer, production authentication and sessions, nine approved screens, persistent audit records, complete appeal review/export workflow, accessibility evidence, operational support controls, UAT execution, and release approval.

## Gen3 — Proactive intelligence

**Status: Planned; separately gated**

The proposed phase adds period comparison, variance decomposition, versioned anomaly detection, and proactive cited findings. It does not introduce autonomous outbound action or production write access.
